About the artist

A quiet practice,
made by hand.

A

The studio is a place for slowing down. I work through layers of paint, leaving traces of decisions, revisions, and time visible in every surface.

The paintings begin with observation but rarely remain literal. A horizon becomes a memory; a shadow becomes a color; a familiar place becomes something imagined.

Works are released in small collections and are available as original pieces. For collectors interested in a specific work, dimensions, framing, shipping, and private viewing can be discussed directly.
